# IqraQuest — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-30_

## Summary

IqraQuest collects no personal data. There is no user account, no
backend server, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Everything
the app knows about you — your name entries, your progress, your
settings, your saved game — stays on your device.

## What we do not do

- We do not create or require a user account.
- We do not operate a backend server. IqraQuest works entirely offline
  after installation, except for the App Store / Google Play purchase
  flow described below.
- We do not show advertising.
- We do not use any advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or fingerprinting
  technique.
- We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.
- We do not know who you are.

## What is stored, and where

All of the following is stored **only on your device**, using standard
local storage (and, for your Premium purchase status specifically,
secure encrypted local storage):

- Player names you type in for a Solo or Family game. These are used
  only to label players during that session and are never transmitted
  anywhere.
- Your game progress: games played, win rate, questions answered,
  category statistics, daily-challenge streak.
- Your in-progress game (so you can resume it after closing the app).
- Your settings: language, dark mode, reduce-motion, sound.
- Whether you have purchased IqraQuest Premium (`iqraquest_full_access`).

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data. There is no cloud backup
and nothing to delete on our end, because we never had a copy.

## Purchases

The one-time Premium purchase (`iqraquest_full_access`) is processed
entirely by Apple's App Store (StoreKit) or Google Play (Play Billing).
IqraQuest never sees your payment details — that information is handled
by Apple/Google under their own privacy policies. IqraQuest only learns
whether the purchase succeeded, so it can unlock Premium content locally
on your device.

## Children

IqraQuest is designed to be usable by children as well as adults (ages
7–99). Because we collect no personal data from anyone, we collect no
personal data from children either. Any action that leaves the app (an
external link, a purchase) is protected by a Parental Gate — a simple
logic question — so a young child cannot trigger it accidentally.

## Notifications

If you enable optional Daily Challenge reminders in Settings, these are
scheduled entirely on your device using local notifications. No
notification data is sent to any server.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the app
update and be dated above.

## Contact

This app has no backend and no account system, so there is no user data
to request, export, or delete beyond uninstalling the app. For questions
about this policy, contact the developer through the App Store / Google
Play listing.
